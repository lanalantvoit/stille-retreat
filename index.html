The Essence

A journey through arctic light, open water, and the deep quiet of the north.

This retreat explores the feeling of stillhet — the particular stillness that settles over you when the landscape is vast and unhurried. Moving from the wild shores of Tromsø through the remote islands of Lofoten, each day invites you to slow down, to notice more, and to return to a quieter pace.

ARCTIC SHORES — ISLAND VILLAGES — OPEN FJORDS — NORTHERN LIGHT

A Journey Through the Far North

We travel through remote arctic landscapes shaped by sea, stone, and shifting sky. The journey unfolds gradually — from the wild coast of Tromsø through the dramatic peaks and fishing villages of Lofoten — creating a sense of movement without urgency.

Each place offers a different atmosphere, yet all share the same underlying stillness that defines the far north.

Coastal Stillness & Open Space

We spend several days by the water, where the landscape opens to sea and sky in every direction. Mornings are slow, afternoons open — with the option to explore the islands on foot, or simply stay present where you are.

There is no pressure to fill time — only the invitation to experience it differently.

Movement & Stillness

Throughout the retreat, Astrid guides daily sessions that combine gentle movement, breath, and quiet awareness. Her approach is simple and responsive — shaped by the group and the environment rather than a fixed structure. The intention is not intensity, but presence.

Time Outdoors

Time outside is an essential part of the experience — walking, light activity, or simply being in the landscape.

The focus is not performance, but immersion: noticing texture, light, and space in a more attentive way.

"Spring carries a quiet sense of beginning — not loud or dramatic, but steady and real."

— Astrid, Founder

Four Exceptional Hotels

Each stay has been chosen for its atmosphere rather than status — places that feel grounded, remote, and deeply connected to their surroundings.

Expect simplicity, warmth, and a sense of space — with good food, natural materials, and views that ask nothing of you but presence.

Thoughtfully Planned

Every part of the journey has been considered — from transitions between places to the rhythm of each day.

Nothing is rushed, and nothing is overfilled. The structure supports the experience, without dominating it.

Arrival & Departure

Getting There

Arrival — Day 1

The journey begins in Oslo, where you spend your first night before flying north. We arrange your onward flight to Tromsø — gateway to the Arctic — where the retreat properly begins the following morning.

Departure — Day 10

Our journey ends with breakfast in Svolvær. You depart from Bodø Airport, a short flight from Lofoten. Connections to Oslo and onward are typically available from mid-morning. Up Norway will assist with any additional planning before or after the retreat.
